THE NEIGHBORHOOD GUESSING GAME

RIDGEWOOD or BUSHWICK?

Think you know your way around town?

Since the dawn of time, the neighboring kingdoms of Ridgewood and Bushwick have lived side by side in peace and harmony. That is… until the arrival of the transplants. These fearsome creatures laid siege to what were once provincial outer-borough enclaves, bringing with them rising rents, matcha lattes, and “experimental” sound collage art.

Can you prove you’re not one of them? How well do you know the lay of the land? You will be presented with eight photos and must correctly identify their locations. Conquer the Transplant Quest to win acclaim, bragging rights, and the chance to enjoy a beer/shot combo with Zohran Mamdani.

Brought to you by the good folks of Ridgewood Crave.
